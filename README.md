# Stuck in Traffic

Get to your office exit in 5 minutes. Good luck.

## How to play
- **Change lanes:** swipe left/right, or press ← → (or A / D)
- **Brake:** hold the BRAKE button, or S / ↓
- **Merge:** only works if your vehicle fits the gap. Miss and you lose 3 seconds.
- **Honk:** Space or the HONK button.
- **Texts:** tap or swipe them away.
- **Exit:** the far right lane near the end. Miss it and you lose.

Pick your ride to set the difficulty: Tiny car (easy), Sedan (medium), Box truck (hard).
Win fast to unlock new horns. Your best runs are saved on your device.

## Run it
Open `index.html` in any browser. No install needed.

## Put it online with GitHub Pages
1. Upload `index.html` (and this README) to a new GitHub repository.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under "Branch," pick `main` and `/ (root)`, then save.
4. After a minute, your game is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/`.

## Tweak the difficulty
Open `index.html` and look for `CONFIG` near the top of the script. Change things like the time limit, how far the exit is, and how cruel the traffic is.
